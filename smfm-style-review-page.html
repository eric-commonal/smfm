FAQ

Membership questions

When do dues renew?

Membership runs on a calendar year. Renewal notices go out in November and access continues uninterrupted if you renew before January 31.

Can I transfer my meeting registration?

Registrations are transferable to another member up to two weeks before the meeting. Contact the membership team to process the change.

How do I update my practice information?

Edit your profile from the member portal. Directory listings refresh within one business day.
